Research identity

From a long-term vision to the experiment on the bench.

Three layers, each narrower than the last. The vision is not tied to any technology; the approach is what makes the work recognizable; the expertise is where the results are today.

  1. 01 · VisionLong-term

    Programmable Molecular Systems

    Molecular systems whose states can be written, gated and read out on demand, in cells, tissues and whole organisms.

    Sets the horizon
  2. 02 · ApproachCore method

    Physical Forces as Molecular Inputs

    Sound, light and mechanical stress reach deep tissue and can be aimed with millimeter precision. I build molecules that treat these forces as instructions.

    Defines the identity
  3. 03 · ExpertiseCurrent

    Ultrasound Mechanochemistry

    Focused ultrasound that activates force-responsive molecules in tumors and in living plants, with imaging to confirm where activation happens.

About

Chemist by training, engineer by practice.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Bioengineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Yun-Sheng Chen at the Beckman Institute.

My work joins acoustic physics, polymer mechanochemistry and molecular engineering. I use focused ultrasound to activate force-responsive molecules deep in tissue, and I am building toward molecular systems that can be programmed entirely by physical inputs.

I trained in chemical biology at the University of Science and Technology of China.
